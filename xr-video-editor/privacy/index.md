# Cutbench Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-22

Cutbench is a video editor for Android XR devices. This policy explains what the app does with information on your device.

## Summary

Cutbench does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal or device information. It has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no internet permission of its own. Everything you edit stays on your device.

## What the app reads

- **Videos and sound files you pick.** Cutbench opens only the files you choose in the Android system file picker. It keeps a permission to read those files so your project still opens the next time; it gives the permission back when a file is no longer used by the project. If a file can only be read for a short time, Cutbench keeps its own copy in the app's private storage, and deletes that copy when the project no longer uses it.
- **Nothing else.** Cutbench does not read your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or any file you did not pick.

## What the app stores, and where

- **Your project** (the list of clips, cuts, titles, music and the last undo steps) is saved in the app's private storage on your device. Other apps cannot read it. It is not backed up to the cloud by Cutbench.
- **Exported videos** are saved to the `Movies/Cutbench` folder of your device storage, where your other apps (for example a gallery) can see them. They are yours; Cutbench never uploads them.

## Permissions

- **Foreground service (data sync).** Lets an export keep running while you look at another app. While it runs, Android may show an "Exporting video" notification.
- **Network state and wake lock.** Requested by the video playback library that Cutbench uses. Cutbench itself makes no network connections; it has no internet permission.

## Purchase

Cutbench is a paid app. The purchase is made and handled entirely by Google Play under Google's own privacy policy; Cutbench itself receives no payment or account details.

## Privacy policy link in the app

The first screen has a "Privacy policy" button that opens this page in your browser. The browser, not Cutbench, connects to the internet for it.

## Sharing

Cutbench shares no information with anyone. No third-party services or software development kits that collect data are included.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling Cutbench deletes the project and its private copies. Exported videos in `Movies/Cutbench` stay on your device until you delete them, for example in your file manager or gallery. The files you imported are never changed or deleted by Cutbench.

## Children

Cutbench is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.
